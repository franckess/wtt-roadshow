WTTUS Roadshow 2026
Saved on this phone

Field rules every day, every city

Cameras

  • DJI: the horizontal master. Establishing shots, controlled walking, stable two-host conversations, interviews.
  • iPhone: candid reactions, close details, passenger-window views, vertical clips, backup.
  • Both: DJI horizontal master first, then a short iPhone vertical only if it’s worth it and there’s time. Never make a guest repeat an answer.

At every stop

  • One wide, one medium action, one close detail, and an honest reaction if there is one.
  • Hold each shot steady for about 5 seconds. Leave room at the start and end.
  • Morning prompt: “What are we looking forward to?” Evening prompt: “What surprised us?”

Sound

  • Mic every conversation and check the levels.
  • Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after each answer.
  • Record about 10 seconds of clean ambient sound at each location, where allowed.

Never film

  • Passports, boarding passes, QR codes, barcodes, room numbers or private itinerary screens.
  • Security or restricted areas, or strangers close up without permission.
  • Anything from the driver’s seat. Roadside shots only from safe, legal stops.

What comes first

  • Travel, rest, booked activities, safety and consent beat coverage.
  • Never present a pending or optional plan as something that happened.
  • Capture a lot. Publish selectively. Bank the rest.

Every night

  • Charge the DJI, phones and mic batteries. Clear storage.
  • Offload, then back up to two separate places and check the copies before wiping a card.
